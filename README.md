McKesson data breach (August 2026)
McKesson, a large healthcare and pharmaceutical distributor, reported unauthorized access to some of its third-party applications. The hacker group Shiny Hunters claimed to have stolen patient data.

What I learned: attackers often get in through third-party systems, not the company's own servers, so securing vendors and the tools you connect matters as much as securing your own network.
